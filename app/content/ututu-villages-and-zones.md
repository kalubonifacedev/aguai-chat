# Ututu People, Boundaries, Villages and Zones

## The Ututu People

The Ututu people are a group of people inhabiting Ututu Clan in Arochukwu Local Government Area of Abia State.

## Original Boundaries of Ututu

- **East:** the Cross River and Isu Clan
- **South:** the Enyong and Itu Rivers / Creeks
- **North:** Ihechiowa Clan
- **West:** Arochukwu Clan, and the Nkana River / Iwerre Clan

## Origin of the Name

The geographical area known as Ututu is named after the founder of the Clan, Mazi Otutu Ezema.

## Villages and Zones

The stratification of Ututu villages is best described by Dr. C. O. Okoreaffia, a renowned anthropologist. According to him, Ututu is an autonomous community of **19 villages**. These villages are zoned into **four anthropological groupings**, which have stood the test of settlement stratification from the very beginning of the Ututu settlement.

The zones, in alphabetical order, are:

### Zone One: Ututu Akasi (5 villages)

1. Amakofia
2. Eziama
3. Obijoma
4. Ohomja
5. Ugwuogo

### Zone Two: Ututu Eleoha (6 villages)

1. Amasa
2. Amaeke
3. Amankwu
4. Amodu
5. Nkpakpi
6. Obiagwulu

### Zone Three: Ututu Umunna Isii (4 villages)

1. Amaebem
2. Obiakang
3. Obialuoko
4. Ukwuakwu

### Zone Four: Ututu Umu Ugwuonyiri (4 villages)

1. Abuma
2. Amaetiti
3. Obiene
4. Ubila
